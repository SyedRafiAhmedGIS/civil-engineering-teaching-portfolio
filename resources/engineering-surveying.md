## Professional context and examination

- [NCEES](https://ncees.org) — the US licensing body for engineers and surveyors; publishes the
  specifications and reference handbooks for the Fundamentals of Surveying (FS) examination.
  The FS exam outline is a strong week-one handout: it connects every topic in the syllabus
  to a visible professional milestone.
- [International Federation of Surveyors (FIG)](https://www.fig.net) — the global federation of
  surveying professional associations, with free commission publications on practice and
  standards. Suitable for advanced learners writing term papers; its working-group
  papers demonstrate how national practice fits into an international framework.

## Reference data and geodetic foundations

- [NOAA National Geodetic Survey](https://geodesy.noaa.gov) — the United States authority for
  the national coordinate system, providing geodetic data, survey control markers, and
  positioning tools. For benchmarks and datum, a worked exercise is to look up real control
  stations near campus and read their datasheets — this makes abstract datums
  concrete.
- [U.S. Geological Survey](https://www.usgs.gov) — the federal science platform for topographic
  maps, elevation models, and hydrography. First-year students download a historical topographic
  quadrangle and compare it with the modern one to understand map revision and scale.
- [National Remote Sensing Centre (NRSC / ISRO)](https://www.nrsc.gov.in) — India's earth
  observation data centre, offering satellite products and thematic maps of the subcontinent.
  For students in South Asia this localises the syllabus: its freely browsable products
  demonstrate how surveyed ground control supports national mapping.

## Mathematics foundations

- [Khan Academy — Geometry](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/geometry) — self-paced units on
  triangles, circles, and coordinate geometry that underpin traversing, curve ranging, and area
  computation. The triangles unit works as remedial work before the traverse calculation
  lecture; students who complete it consistently make fewer angular-closure errors.

## Skills practice

- [NPTEL](https://nptel.ac.in) — national video course platform with surveying modules under
  Domains → Civil Engineering. Its surveying series is recommended to students who miss a field
  session; the instrument-handling videos are a reasonable pre-lecture substitute.
