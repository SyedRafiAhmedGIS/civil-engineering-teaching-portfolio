# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

- **Be respectful.** Critique content, never people. No harassment, insults,
  or personal attacks.
- **Be honest.** Report issues you have actually reproduced; describe
  resources you have actually opened.
- **Respect ownership.** Never suggest links to pirated standard texts or
  other people's copyrighted material; never copy another repository's
  wording into this one.
- **Protect privacy.** No student identifiers or personal data may be shared
  in issues, pull requests, or example data.

## Scope

Applies to this repository's issues, pull requests, discussions, and any
project space representing it.

## Enforcement

The maintainer (Syed Rafi Ahmed, github.com/SyedRafiAhmedGIS) may edit or
remove contributions and ban accounts that repeatedly violate this code.
Report problems by opening an issue or contacting the maintainer through
GitHub; reports are reviewed and answered.
