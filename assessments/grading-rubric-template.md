# Grading Rubric Template (Analytic)

Adaptable to laboratory reports, projects and essays in engineering coursework. Replace the
weights to match the assessment; keep the levels and descriptors intact so standards stay
consistent across graders and semesters. Total: 100 points, converted to the assessment's
weight in the course outline.

**Assessment being marked:** ________ · **Course:** ________ · **Marks:** ____ / 100

| Criterion | Weight | Excellent (full marks) | Good (~75%) | Satisfactory (~60%) | Needs improvement (<40%) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Technical accuracy** | 30% | All calculations correct with units; methods fit the task; no conceptual errors | Minor numerical slips; method sound; units nearly always present | Some method errors; core idea visible; units inconsistent | Fundamental misunderstanding of method or physics |
| **2. Methodology & data handling** | 25% | Data provenance clear; synthetic vs measured labelled; equipment handled per standard; repeats where needed | Data recorded fully; minor gaps in provenance or repeats | Data usable but gaps; some unexplained values | Missing or unexplained data; procedures not followed |
| **3. Analysis & interpretation** | 20% | Statistics applied correctly; results compared against the right rule; uncertainty discussed honestly | Correct statistics; comparison present; limited depth of uncertainty discussion | Basic statistics; shallow comparison; uncertainty ignored | Results presented without interpretation or against wrong criteria |
| **4. Presentation & structure** | 15% | Logical flow; figures/tables numbered, captioned, referenced; within length limits | Clear structure; minor formatting lapses | Readable but disorganised; figures uncaptioned | Disordered; missing figures/tables |
| **5. Professional & teamwork conduct** | 10% | Safety observed; deadlines met; data sheet signed and honest; team roles stated and real | Small lapses in any one of safety/deadline/signing | Noticeable lapses; honesty intact | Safety violations, fabricated data, or free-riding |

## How to use

1. Mark each criterion at the level whose descriptor dominates the work; interpolate
   within a band only with a written comment justifying the position.
2. Every non-full-marks score requires at least one actionable sentence of feedback.
3. Boundary cases (two bands plausible) get a second marker or moderation sample.
4. Fabricated data or safety violations trigger the academic-integrity process rather than
   a mere rubric deduction - record the evidence and follow policy.

## Mapping to outcomes

When using this rubric for a course assessment, note which course outcomes each criterion
provides evidence for (see the course's `outcomes-assessment-map.md`). Criterion 1 and 3
typically carry the cognitive evidence; criterion 2 and 5 the professional and psychomotor
evidence.
