# Engineering Surveying — Outcomes-to-Assessment Map

| Course outcome | Quizzes (10%) | Field notebooks (15%) | Traverse project (20%) | Midterm (15%) | Final (40%) | Evidence level |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CO1 Principles & error handling | Q1, Q2 | 4% | 2% | 5% | 8% | Understand / Apply |
| CO2 Levelling & misclosure | Q2 | 5% | - | 4% | 9% | Apply |
| CO3 Total station & traverse | Q3 | 4% | 12% | 3% | 9% | Apply / Analyse |
| CO4 Contours, areas, volumes | Q4 | 2% | 4% | 3% | 8% | Apply / Analyse |
| CO5 GNSS & setting out | Q5 | - | 2% | - | 6% | Understand / Apply |

## Design notes

- The traverse project is the capstone evidence for CO3 and deliberately integrates CO1-CO4:
  a traverse cannot close unless errors have been handled correctly from week 2 onwards.
- Field notebooks carry meaningful weight (15%) because booking discipline is a
  professional-defining habit; three scheduled checks keep the feedback loop short.
- The final examination re-tests every outcome numerically; no essay-only questions.

## Continuous improvement loop

Per-outcome averages are computed at semester end; any outcome below 60% triggers a change
in the paired field activity for the next cohort and a note in the course file. Where
instrument availability limits a skill (e.g. RTK), the map is honest about the evidence
level achieved and the gap is addressed by demonstration plus case study rather than
pretending full psychomotor coverage.
